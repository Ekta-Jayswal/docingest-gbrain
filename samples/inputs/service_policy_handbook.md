# Customer Service Policy Handbook

Rule 1 covers returns. Agents confirm the customer's identity, check the order history in OrderHub, and record every decision about returns in CareDesk with the reason code. If the customer disagrees, the agent offers escalation to a supervisor within one working day, and the supervisor replies in writing. Exceptions to rule 1 need approval from the Head of Support Operations and are reviewed monthly for patterns.
