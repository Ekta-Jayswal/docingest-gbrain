SOW number: SOW-2026-118
Effective date: 2026-11-03
Governing agreement: Master Services Agreement MSA-2024-031

1. Background

Calder Freight runs its operational reporting on an on-premises warehouse that is near the end of its support life. Calder Freight has engaged Brightwater Analytics to move the warehouse, its 140 nightly loads and the finance reports built on it to a managed cloud data platform, and to retire the old warehouse once the new one is accepted.

3. Scope

3.1 In scope

  • Migration of 140 nightly data loads to the new platform
  • Rebuild of 32 finance reports on the new platform
  • Parallel run of old and new platforms for one month-end close
  • Decommissioning of the on-premises warehouse

3.2 Out of scope

  • New reports or dashboards beyond the 32 listed in Appendix A
  • Changes to source systems
  • Ongoing platform support after acceptance
